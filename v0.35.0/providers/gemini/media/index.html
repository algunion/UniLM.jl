Language models · Google Gemini

Images, speech, embeddings & the compatible endpoint

Image generation

With service=GEMINIServiceEndpoint, generate_image and edit_image call generateContent on an image model, gemini-nano-banana-2.1 unless the request names another, with image output (responseModalities: ["IMAGE"]). The request's model field stays "" until the request is sent, and the model goes in the URL. Size the image with two fields of Google's:

  • aspect_ratio: "1:1", "1:4", "4:1", "1:8", "8:1", "2:3", "3:2", "3:4", "4:3", "4:5", "5:4", "9:16", "16:9" or "21:9"; unset, the model's own choice;
  • resolution: "512", "1K", "2K" or "4K", of which gemini-nano-banana-2.1 takes "1K", "2K" and "4K", the "output resolutions (default 1K)" of its model page (Nano Banana 2.1).

They go in generationConfig.imageConfig, and safety_identifier in the request label (Request options). Two more go in generationConfig.responseFormat.image, Gemini's "Configuration for image output format":

  • output_format: "jpeg", its mimeType IMAGE_JPEG, the one format Gemini's API reference lists; another is refused before the request. Gemini answers JPEG unasked, and on 17 November 2026 it answered IMAGE_PNG, a value its reference does not list, with a JPEG too;
  • delivery: "inline" or "uri", "The delivery mode for the image output": the image in the reply, or a URI to fetch it from, which becomes the image's url. On 17 November 2026 gemini-nano-banana-2.1 answered "uri" with the image inline, and the result carries it as base64 as it does any image.

responseFormat.image takes an aspect ratio and a size too, the controls that aspect_ratio and resolution set, and those stay in imageConfig: one name for each control. On 17 November 2026 Gemini took the two objects side by side.

A request returns one image, so n takes only 1, and the image fields generateContent has no counterpart for — size, quality, background, output_compression, moderation, OpenAI's GPT-Image-2.6 controls (complexity, frame_count, frame_duration_ms and svg), stream and partial_images, and an edit's mask, markup and input_fidelity — are refused before the request (Limits and refusals). Gemini has no partial images to stream: on 17 November 2026 its streamGenerateContent answered an image request with one event that held the whole image. An image request is not recorded, so its output here is quoted from a live run:

result = generate_image("A lighthouse on a rocky coast at dusk, oil painting";
                        service=GEMINIServiceEndpoint, aspect_ratio="16:9")
result isa ImageSuccess || error("the image request failed: $result")
save_image(image_data(result)[1], "lighthouse.jpg")
# Generated on 25 October 2026 with UniLM's development branch after 0.26.0 on gemini-nano-banana-2.1:
# "lighthouse.jpg"

parts = result.response.raw["candidates"][1]["content"]["parts"]
[p["inlineData"]["mimeType"] for p in parts if haskey(p, "inlineData")]
# 1-element Vector{String}:
#  "image/jpeg"

u = token_usage(result)
(; u.prompt_tokens, u.completion_tokens, u.reasoning_tokens)
# (prompt_tokens = 12, completion_tokens = 2224, reasoning_tokens = 793)

The reply's image parts are the result's images, in order, each as base64 in b64_json, which image_data lists and save_image decodes into a file — or, for a part Gemini delivers by URI (fileData), as its url; their MIME type is in the reply, result.response.raw. Text the model sends with an image is its revised_prompt, and the interim images of the model's thinking are not results. A reply with no image — a filter stopped it, say — is an ImageCallError naming the finish reason (IMAGE_SAFETY, NO_IMAGE, …), and a reply has no creation time, so result.response.created is the time it arrived. "All generated images include a SynthID watermark" (image generation).

An edit sends its input images inline, before the prompt, and decodes the same way; it reads them from files, so an ImageRef, OpenAI's image by reference, is refused:

edited = edit_image("lighthouse.jpg", "Make it a winter night with snow on the rocks";
                    service=GEMINIServiceEndpoint)
edited isa ImageSuccess || error("the edit failed: $edited")
length(image_data(edited)), edited.response.data[1].revised_prompt
# (1, nothing)

eu = token_usage(edited)
(; eu.prompt_tokens, eu.completion_tokens, eu.reasoning_tokens)
# (prompt_tokens = 1131, completion_tokens = 2325, reasoning_tokens = 858)

token_usage reads the reply's usage as a Chat's (First call), its thinking counted as output; the image is output too — 1,120 of the generation's tokens above, by the candidatesTokensDetails of its raw usage, as Google says "Output images at 1K (1024x1024px) consume 1120 tokens" (pricing). image_usage gives the same counts with those apart, as on OpenAI: the input's text and image tokens, by modality (promptTokensDetails), and the output's image tokens and the rest, its text and thinking, which Google prices apart ("$30.00 (images)", "$7.50 (text and thinking)" per million output tokens for gemini-nano-banana-2.1, 17 November 2026). UniLM does not price images: estimated_cost throws ArgumentError for an image result unless you pass model=, which it prices by a pricing row of your own, and estimates at 0.0, with a warning, where no row names it. The options every provider shares are on Image Generation.

Audio

speak with service=GEMINIServiceEndpoint reads text aloud through Gemini TTS: a generateContent request to gemini-3.8-flash-tts, unless model names another, in the voice Kore, unless voice names another, sent again after a transient status or a transport failure, as a Chat is (Timeouts & Retries). Gemini 3.8 TTS takes "a prebuilt voice name, an Extended Voice Library ID, a custom Voice design ID (voice_...), or a Voice replication ID (voice_..., or optional stateless voicekey_...)" (speech generation): one of the 30 studio voices that page lists goes as a prebuilt voice, any other as the request's voice. The Extended Voice Library's hundreds of voices are listed by GET /v1beta/voices, which list_voices reads, each a Voice whose id (such as "achernar" or "am-et-assistant-1") speak takes; create_voice designs or replicates one of your own (Custom voices). A voice is sent as given, and Gemini answers a name it does not have. UniLM asks for 24 kHz, 16-bit mono PCM and returns a SpeechSuccess: a WAV file with response_format="wav", the default, which UniLM wraps in a 44-byte header, or the samples with "pcm" (content_type "audio/pcm"). Speech is not recorded, so its output here is quoted from a live run:

s = speak("The bridge reopens on Monday morning."; service=GEMINIServiceEndpoint,
          instructions="calm and clear, like a radio announcement")
s isa SpeechSuccess || error("speech failed: $s")
s.content_type, length(s.audio)
# Spoken on 25 October 2026 with UniLM's development branch after 0.26.0 on gemini-3.8-flash-tts:
# ("audio/wav", 142124)

(length(s.audio) - 44) / (24_000 * 2)   # seconds of 16-bit mono at 24 kHz, after the WAV header
# 2.96

save_audio(s, "bridge.wav")

"Gemini 3.8 TTS treats the text field strictly as a verbatim transcript", and the delivery goes in the speech style, speech_metadata.style: "emotions, delivery style, prosody, pacing, and volume" (speech generation). instructions become that style, and the pace is part of it ("speaking slowly"): Gemini TTS has no speed, so any speed is refused, as are a blank voice, a format other than "wav" and "pcm" and an empty input (Limits and refusals). language is the BCP-47 tag the text is read in (speechConfig.languageCode; Gemini answered "xx-ZZ" with HTTP 400 "Invalid language_code").

A dialogue reads several voices in one request: SpeechTurns as input and each speaker's voice in speakers. Each turn is a part with its speaker and style in speech_metadata, as the guide asks — "pass one part per speaker turn with speech_metadata.speaker" — and the voices go as multiSpeakerVoiceConfig:

s = speak([SpeechTurn("Joe", "Is the bridge open?"), SpeechTurn("Jane", "From Monday morning."; style="reassuring")];
          service=GEMINIServiceEndpoint, speakers=["Joe" => "Puck", "Jane" => "Kore"], language="en-US")

A turn whose speaker has no voice in speakers, a speaker named twice, and a dialogue with a voice or instructions are refused before the request. The guide's limits say multi-speaker speech "supports up to 2 speakers using prebuilt voices"; Gemini spoke three speakers, and a designed voice beside a prebuilt one, on 17 November 2026, so UniLM refuses neither.

A reply whose candidate did not finish with STOP — MAX_TOKENS, SAFETY, … — or that has no candidate, a blocked prompt, is an AudioFailure with status 200 and the reply as response; an HTTP error status is an AudioFailure too. A reply without audio, a timeout and a cancel are an AudioCallError. Speech is not priced: token_usage and estimated_cost throw for a SpeechSuccess. speak refuses GEMINIOpenAIServiceEndpoint, whose wire has no speech route.

transcribe with service=GEMINIServiceEndpoint sends the audio file inline to Google's transcription model, gemini-3.5-transcribe unless model names another, in one generateContent request (transcribe), retried after a transient status as speech is. Its options go in generationConfig.audioTranscriptionConfig: languages (BCP-47 codes such as "en-US") as languageCodes, keywords (up to 1,000) as customVocabulary, diarization = true, timestamp_granularities = ["word"] as wordTimestamp, mode ("verbatim" or "smart") and, in smart mode, source_text. Each part of the reply is one speaker turn, a TranscriptSegment labelled "spk:0", "spk:1", …; the words are TranscriptWords with their speaker and their offsets in seconds:

r = transcribe("call.wav"; service=GEMINIServiceEndpoint, diarization=true, timestamp_granularities=["word"])
r isa TranscriptionSuccess && foreach(w -> println(w.speaker, " ", w.start_time, " ", w.text), r.words)

Gemini rejects custom vocabulary beside diarization or word timestamps (HTTP 400 "customvocabulary is incompatible with diarization.", 17 November 2026) and smart mode beside either; those, and the options Gemini's transcription does not take (prompt, temperature, `responseformat,include,chunkingstrategy, known speakers,stream), throwArgumentErrorbefore any request. The Interactions API takes the same options astranscriptionconfigand returned the same transcript; UniLM uses generateContent, which stores nothing. Transcription is not priced: [tokenusage](@ref) throws for a [TranscriptionSuccess](@ref), whoseraw["usageMetadata"]keeps Gemini's count.GEMINIOpenAIServiceEndpoint` has no transcription. The verbs every provider shares are on [Audio API](@ref audioapi).

Embeddings

UniLM embeds text with Gemini on both endpoints. GEMINIServiceEndpoint sends Google's own embedding methods (embeddings reference): models/{model}:embedContent for one input, models/{model}:batchEmbedContents for a vector of them, up to 100 — the API answered 101 with HTTP 400 "at most 100 requests can be in one batch" on 17 November 2026, and UniLM refuses more before the request. GEMINIOpenAIServiceEndpoint sends Google's OpenAI-compatible /v1beta/openai/embeddings. The default model of both is gemini-embedding-2, which takes "Input token limit 8,192" and an output dimension that is "Flexible, supports: 128 - 3072, Recommended: 768, 1536, 3072" (Gemini Embedding 2); UniLM sends dimensions unchecked (the native API answered 4000 with HTTP 400, naming the range it takes, 1 to 3072).

emb = Embeddings(["How long do refunds take?", "Is shipping free within the EU?"];
                 service=GEMINIOpenAIServiceEndpoint, task=:search_query, dimensions=768)
result = embeddingrequest!(emb)
length.(embedding_vectors(result)), result.usage

gemini-embedding-2 reads a task from the text itself — "You cannot use the task_type field for the gemini-embedding-2 model. Instead, include the task as an instruction in your prompt" (embeddings) — so task writes Google's prompt in front of each text: "task: search result | query: " for :search_query, "title: … | text: " for :document, with an EmbeddingInput's title (none without one), and the others as Embeddings lists them. A text without a task goes as it is. Google normalizes every size on gemini-embedding-2: "While the default 3072-dimension embeddings are always normalized, Gemini Embedding 2 also auto-normalizes truncated dimensions (e.g., 768, 1536)"; only on gemini-embedding-001 "you must manually normalize non-3072 dimensions" (embeddings).

The same request on GEMINIServiceEndpoint goes to batchEmbedContents, one request per text, each naming its model:

emb = Embeddings(["How long do refunds take?", "Is shipping free within the EU?"];
                 service=GEMINIServiceEndpoint, task=:search_query, dimensions=768)
result = embeddingrequest!(emb)
length.(embedding_vectors(result)), result.usage.prompt_tokens

On GEMINIServiceEndpoint, gemini-embedding-2 reads its task from the text too: a taskType or a title sent beside it left its vector unchanged on 17 November 2026, so UniLM sends neither and writes the prompt. gemini-embedding-001 takes the task as its taskType instead ("For gemini-embedding-001, you can specify the task_type in the embedContent method"), with no prompt in the text:

tasktaskType
:search_queryRETRIEVAL_QUERY
:documentRETRIEVAL_DOCUMENT, with an EmbeddingInput's title as title
:question_answeringQUESTION_ANSWERING
:fact_checkingFACT_VERIFICATION
:code_retrievalCODE_RETRIEVAL_QUERY
:classificationCLASSIFICATION
:clusteringCLUSTERING
:sentence_similaritySEMANTIC_SIMILARITY
:none, or noneno task type

A title beside any other task is refused before the request: the API answered one with HTTP 400 "the model does not support the title parameter unless the tasktype is RETRIEVALDOCUMENT". The OpenAI-compatible endpoint has no task field, so there gemini-embedding-001 takes task = :none or none. UniLM sends taskType, title and outputDimensionality at the request's top level, as the REST examples of Google's embeddings guide do, though the API's discovery document marks them deprecated in favour of embedContentConfig: on 17 November 2026 gemini-embedding-001 ignored all three inside embedContentConfig (its vector came back with 3072 dimensions, unchanged by the task) and honoured them at the top, and gemini-embedding-2 honoured outputDimensionality in both places. embedContentConfig's other options are not sent: autoTruncate would "silently truncate the input content if it's longer than the maximum sequence length", and UniLM returns no vector of part of an input; documentOcr and audioTrackExtraction apply to documents and video, which UniLM does not embed. An Embeddings' user has no field there and is refused.

The native reply carries usageMetadata for gemini-embedding-2, whose promptTokenCount is the result's usage, priced at the model's row as on the compatible endpoint; gemini-embedding-001's replies carried none on 17 November 2026, so its result is estimated at 0.0, with one warning per model. A reply without the vectors it owes is an EmbeddingCallError, never zeros. Many requests at half price go in a batch (Batches of embeddings).

Both endpoints take text: an EmbeddingInput with an image or a sound clip is refused before the request (Limits and refusals), though gemini-embedding-2 embeds images, audio, video and PDFs, which UniLM does not send it. The compatible endpoint's reply carries no usage, so result.usage is nothing and estimated_cost gives 0.0, with one warning per model, though Google lists a "Text input price" for the model (pricing). That reply leaves the index out of its first row, which UniLM reads as row 0. gemini-embedding-001 is served until its shutdown on "May 14, 2028", with gemini-embedding-2 the replacement Google names (deprecations), and "The embedding spaces between gemini-embedding-001 and gemini-embedding-2 are incompatible" (embeddings): re-embed your data to move.

The OpenAI-compatible endpoint

GEMINIOpenAIServiceEndpoint speaks Google's OpenAI-compatible endpoint, https://generativelanguage.googleapis.com/v1beta/openai/chat/completions, with the key as Authorization: Bearer. Google says its support "is still in beta while we extend feature support" (OpenAI compatibility). A Chat reaches the same models, with the default model of GEMINIServiceEndpoint:

chat = Chat(service=GEMINIOpenAIServiceEndpoint)

There UniLM is an OpenAI-compatible client, and of these pages only these rules hold:

  • Sampling and tiers. temperature and top_p follow the rule of Request options, and service_tier takes "standard", "flex" and "priority", the tiers Google matches "in name and logic" there; another value is refused. The replies named no billed tier on 22 October 2026, so a result records the one its request asked for (a tier a reply names is kept): "flex" is priced at the Flex rates and "priority" at the Priority rates, an upper bound, since a downgraded Priority request bills at the standard rate and this wire did not say so.
  • Thinking levels. reasoning_effort is checked against the model's row of the thinking table, as on generateContent, and an effort the row lacks is refused before the request is sent. Google's compatibility page maps "minimal" to low on Gemini 4, but the endpoint answered "minimal" on gemini-4-hydrogen, gemini-4-carbon, gemini-4-argon and gemini-3.8-flash with HTTP 400 "Thinking level MINIMAL is not supported for this model", and took "none" on gemini-3.8-flash and thought anyway (16 November 2026). A gemini-2.5- model takes a thinking budget there, which "none" turns off, so its effort goes as set, as does an effort for an id no row names.
  • A context cache. cached_content goes as extra_body.google.cached_content, the field Google's compatibility page names, under the rules of Context caching: a system message, tools or a tool_choice beside it, and a Gemini 4 model, are refused before the request (this wire answered the first two with the same HTTP 400 as generateContent on 17 November 2026). A reply that read the cache reports it in prompt_tokens_details.cached_tokens, read as cached_tokens.
  • Everything else is sent as set. No other check runs: prompt_cache_options, a request that ends with an assistant turn and the other refusals of Limits and refusals reach Google, which answered the first two with HTTP 400 on 22 October 2026, returned as an LLMFailure.
  • No thinking; signatures go back. UniLM keeps no provider content from this wire: reasoning_text gives nothing. A function call's thought signature, which this wire gives in the call's extra_content.google.thought_signature, stays on its ToolCall (thought_signature) and goes back with the call, as Google requires ("Gemini 3 supports OpenAI compatibility for thought signatures in chat completion APIs"). A two-turn tool loop on gemini-3.8-flash, streamed and not, was answered on 29 October 2026; without the signature, its second request was answered HTTP 400.
  • Usage. A stream asks for its usage, stream_options = {"include_usage": true}, when the Chat sets none, so a streamed turn is priced. The endpoint counts the thinking only in total_tokens (gemini-4-hydrogen at "xhigh": 38 prompt, 5 completion and 124 total tokens, on 16 November 2026), so the tokens a total holds beyond the prompt and the completion join completion_tokens and reasoning_tokens and bill at the output rate, as generateContent's thoughts do; a reply that names its reasoning tokens keeps that count, and a total below the prompt and the completion is kept as reported, with a warning. The usage counted no audio apart on 23 October 2026, so a clip is priced at the text rate, a lower bound on the four models with an audio price of their own, for which such a request warns once per model.
  • No continuation, no other verbs. A long reply is not continued (Long generations), though a gemini-4- model keeps its 20-minute default bounds; respond, list_models, speak and the image verbs refuse the endpoint, and embeddingrequest! is its own job (Embeddings).

Recorded on 25 October 2026 on gemini-embedding-2, through Google's OpenAI-compatible endpoint, with UniLM's development branch after 0.26.0 and the Gemini API at v1beta.
