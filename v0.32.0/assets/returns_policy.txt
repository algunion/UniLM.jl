Harbor & Pine Outfitters: returns and repairs policy

Returns. A customer may return any unworn item within 45 days of delivery. Items bought
on clearance can be exchanged but not refunded. Gift cards cannot be returned.

Refunds. A refund goes back to the original payment method within 5 business days of
the return being received at the Portland warehouse. Return shipping is free for
members of the Harbor Club and costs a flat 7 dollars for everyone else.

Repairs. Jackets and packs carry a 2-year repair warranty from the date of purchase.
A repair under warranty is free; outside the warranty the workshop quotes a price
before any work starts. Repairs take about 3 weeks.

Contact. Questions about a return or a repair go to care@harborandpine.example, which
answers within one working day.
