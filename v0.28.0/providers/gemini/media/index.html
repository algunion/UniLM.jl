Language models · Google Gemini

Images, speech, embeddings & the compatible endpoint

Image generation

With service=GEMINIServiceEndpoint, generate_image and edit_image call generateContent on an image model, gemini-nano-banana-2.1 unless the request names another, with image output (responseModalities: ["IMAGE"]). The request's model field stays "" until the request is sent, and the model goes in the URL. Size the image with two fields of Google's:

  • aspect_ratio: "1:1", "1:4", "4:1", "1:8", "8:1", "2:3", "3:2", "3:4", "4:3", "4:5", "5:4", "9:16", "16:9" or "21:9"; unset, the model's own choice;
  • resolution: "512", "1K", "2K" or "4K", of which gemini-nano-banana-2.1 takes "1K", "2K" and "4K", the "output resolutions (default 1K)" of its model page (Nano Banana 2.1).

They go in generationConfig.imageConfig, and safety_identifier in the request label (Request options). A request returns one image, so n takes only 1, and the image fields generateContent has no counterpart for — size, quality, background, output_format, output_compression, moderation, and an edit's mask and input_fidelity — are refused before the request (Limits and refusals). An image request is not recorded, so its output here is quoted from a live run:

result = generate_image("A lighthouse on a rocky coast at dusk, oil painting";
                        service=GEMINIServiceEndpoint, aspect_ratio="16:9")
result isa ImageSuccess || error("the image request failed: $result")
save_image(image_data(result)[1], "lighthouse.jpg")
# Generated on 25 October 2026 with UniLM's development branch after 0.26.0 on gemini-nano-banana-2.1:
# "lighthouse.jpg"

parts = result.response.raw["candidates"][1]["content"]["parts"]
[p["inlineData"]["mimeType"] for p in parts if haskey(p, "inlineData")]
# 1-element Vector{String}:
#  "image/jpeg"

u = token_usage(result)
(; u.prompt_tokens, u.completion_tokens, u.reasoning_tokens)
# (prompt_tokens = 12, completion_tokens = 2224, reasoning_tokens = 793)

The reply's image parts are the result's images, in order, each as base64 in b64_json, which image_data lists and save_image decodes into a file; their MIME type is in the reply, result.response.raw. Text the model sends with an image is its revised_prompt, and the interim images of the model's thinking are not results. A reply with no image — a filter stopped it, say — is an ImageCallError naming the finish reason (IMAGE_SAFETY, NO_IMAGE, …), and a reply has no creation time, so result.response.created is the time it arrived. "All generated images include a SynthID watermark" (image generation).

An edit sends its input images inline, before the prompt, and decodes the same way:

edited = edit_image("lighthouse.jpg", "Make it a winter night with snow on the rocks";
                    service=GEMINIServiceEndpoint)
edited isa ImageSuccess || error("the edit failed: $edited")
length(image_data(edited)), edited.response.data[1].revised_prompt
# (1, nothing)

eu = token_usage(edited)
(; eu.prompt_tokens, eu.completion_tokens, eu.reasoning_tokens)
# (prompt_tokens = 1131, completion_tokens = 2325, reasoning_tokens = 858)

token_usage reads the reply's usage as a Chat's (First call), its thinking counted as output; the image is output too — 1,120 of the generation's tokens above, by the candidatesTokensDetails of its raw usage, as Google says "Output images at 1K (1024x1024px) consume 1120 tokens" (pricing). UniLM does not price images: estimated_cost throws ArgumentError for an image result unless you pass model=, which it prices by a pricing row of your own, and estimates at 0.0, with a warning, where no row names it. The options every provider shares are on Image Generation.

Audio

speak with service=GEMINIServiceEndpoint reads text aloud through Gemini TTS: one generateContent request to gemini-3.8-flash-tts, unless model names another, in the voice Kore, unless voice names another. Gemini 3.8 TTS takes "a prebuilt voice name, an Extended Voice Library ID, a custom Voice design ID (voice_...), or a Voice replication ID (voice_..., or optional stateless voicekey_...)" (speech generation): one of the 30 studio voices that page lists goes as a prebuilt voice, any other as the request's voice. The Extended Voice Library's hundreds of voices are listed by GET /v1beta/voices, not by UniLM, so a voice is sent as given, and Gemini answers a name it does not have. UniLM asks for 24 kHz, 16-bit mono PCM and returns a SpeechSuccess: a WAV file with response_format="wav", the default, which UniLM wraps in a 44-byte header, or the samples with "pcm" (content_type "audio/pcm"). Speech is not recorded, so its output here is quoted from a live run:

s = speak("The bridge reopens on Monday morning."; service=GEMINIServiceEndpoint,
          instructions="calm and clear, like a radio announcement")
s isa SpeechSuccess || error("speech failed: $s")
s.content_type, length(s.audio)
# Spoken on 25 October 2026 with UniLM's development branch after 0.26.0 on gemini-3.8-flash-tts:
# ("audio/wav", 142124)

(length(s.audio) - 44) / (24_000 * 2)   # seconds of 16-bit mono at 24 kHz, after the WAV header
# 2.96

save_audio(s, "bridge.wav")

"Gemini 3.8 TTS treats the text field strictly as a verbatim transcript", and the delivery goes in the speech style, speech_metadata.style: "emotions, delivery style, prosody, pacing, and volume" (speech generation). instructions become that style, and the pace is part of it ("speaking slowly"): Gemini TTS has no speed, so any speed is refused, as are a blank voice, a format other than "wav" and "pcm" and an empty input (Limits and refusals).

A reply whose candidate did not finish with STOP — MAX_TOKENS, SAFETY, … — or that has no candidate, a blocked prompt, is an AudioFailure with status 200 and the reply as response; an HTTP error status is an AudioFailure too. A reply without audio, a timeout and a cancel are an AudioCallError. Speech is not priced: token_usage and estimated_cost throw for a SpeechSuccess. speak refuses GEMINIOpenAIServiceEndpoint, whose wire has no speech route.

transcribe refuses both endpoints: neither has the transcription capability. Google serves transcription with a model of its own, gemini-3.5-transcribe (transcribe), which UniLM does not wrap. To get the words of a clip, send it as an AudioAttachment and ask for them, as Attachments does. The verbs every provider shares are on Audio API.

Embeddings

UniLM embeds text with Gemini through GEMINIOpenAIServiceEndpoint, on Google's OpenAI-compatible /v1beta/openai/embeddings; GEMINIServiceEndpoint has no embeddings, and Google's native embedding methods are not wrapped. The default model is gemini-embedding-2, which takes "Input token limit 8,192" and an output dimension that is "Flexible, supports: 128 - 3072, Recommended: 768, 1536, 3072" (Gemini Embedding 2); UniLM sends dimensions unchecked.

emb = Embeddings(["How long do refunds take?", "Is shipping free within the EU?"];
                 service=GEMINIOpenAIServiceEndpoint, task=:search_query, dimensions=768)
result = embeddingrequest!(emb)
length.(embedding_vectors(result)), result.usage

gemini-embedding-2 reads a task from the text itself — "You cannot use the task_type field for the gemini-embedding-2 model. Instead, include the task as an instruction in your prompt" (embeddings) — so task writes Google's prompt in front of each text: "task: search result | query: " for :search_query, "title: … | text: " for :document, with an EmbeddingInput's title (none without one), and the others as Embeddings lists them. A text without a task goes as it is. Google normalizes every size on gemini-embedding-2: "While the default 3072-dimension embeddings are always normalized, Gemini Embedding 2 also auto-normalizes truncated dimensions (e.g., 768, 1536)"; only on gemini-embedding-001 "you must manually normalize non-3072 dimensions" (embeddings).

The endpoint takes text: an EmbeddingInput with an image or a sound clip is refused before the request (Limits and refusals). Its reply carries no usage, so result.usage is nothing and estimated_cost gives 0.0, with one warning per model, though Google lists a "Text input price" for the model (pricing). The reply leaves the index out of its first row, which UniLM reads as row 0. gemini-embedding-001 is served until its shutdown on "May 14, 2028", with gemini-embedding-2 the replacement Google names (deprecations), and "The embedding spaces between gemini-embedding-001 and gemini-embedding-2 are incompatible" (embeddings): re-embed your data to move.

The OpenAI-compatible endpoint

GEMINIOpenAIServiceEndpoint speaks Google's OpenAI-compatible endpoint, https://generativelanguage.googleapis.com/v1beta/openai/chat/completions, with the key as Authorization: Bearer. Google says its support "is still in beta while we extend feature support" (OpenAI compatibility). A Chat reaches the same models, with the default model of GEMINIServiceEndpoint:

chat = Chat(service=GEMINIOpenAIServiceEndpoint)

There UniLM is an OpenAI-compatible client, and of these pages only these rules hold:

  • Sampling and tiers. temperature and top_p follow the rule of Request options, and service_tier takes "standard", "flex" and "priority", the tiers Google matches "in name and logic" there; another value is refused. The replies named no billed tier on 22 October 2026, so a result records the one its request asked for (a tier a reply names is kept): "flex" is priced at the Flex rates and "priority" at the Priority rates, an upper bound, since a downgraded Priority request bills at the standard rate and this wire did not say so.
  • Everything else is sent as set. No other check runs: reasoning_effort goes as it is, Google mapping it to a thinking level, and prompt_cache_options, a request that ends with an assistant turn and the other refusals of Limits and refusals reach Google, which answered the first two with HTTP 400 on 22 October 2026, returned as an LLMFailure.
  • No thinking; signatures go back. UniLM keeps no provider content from this wire: reasoning_text gives nothing. A function call's thought signature, which this wire gives in the call's extra_content.google.thought_signature, stays on its ToolCall (thought_signature) and goes back with the call, as Google requires ("Gemini 3 supports OpenAI compatibility for thought signatures in chat completion APIs"). A two-turn tool loop on gemini-3.8-flash, streamed and not, was answered on 29 October 2026; without the signature, its second request was answered HTTP 400.
  • Usage. A stream asks for its usage, stream_options = {"include_usage": true}, when the Chat sets none, so a streamed turn is priced. The usage counted no audio apart on 23 October 2026, so a clip is priced at the text rate, a lower bound on the four models with an audio price of their own, for which such a request warns once per model.
  • No continuation, no other verbs. A long reply is not continued (Long generations), though a gemini-4- model keeps its 20-minute default bounds; respond, list_models, speak and the image verbs refuse the endpoint, and embeddingrequest! is its own job (Embeddings).

Recorded on 25 October 2026 on gemini-embedding-2, through Google's OpenAI-compatible endpoint, with UniLM's development branch after 0.26.0 and the Gemini API at v1beta.
